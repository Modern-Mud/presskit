## Short description

Modern Mud is a dark-fantasy MMORPG where NPCs remember what you did, eight
gods judge you and any player can block a pass and charge a toll.

## Long description

Remember when games had soul? Before waypoints told you where to walk. Before
every NPC was a vending machine. Before "choices" meant picking dialogue that
led to the same ending.

There was a time when you had to think. When the world didn't care if you were
ready. When getting lost was part of the journey, and discovering a secret felt
like uncovering forbidden knowledge.

Modern Mud is a return to that era, rebuilt for those who refuse to forget.

## Features

- **Tactical combat with real consequences.** Turn-based decisions in
  real-time battles. Defeat leaves you unconscious at your enemy's mercy, but
  your gear stays yours.
- **The Toll System.** Block any passage. Name your price. Watch them pay,
  fight, or flee.
- **NPCs that live, not just exist.** They wake, work, dream and die. They have
  friends who protect them, enemies who hunt them, and successors who inherit
  their grudges.
- **Divine judgment.** Eight gods observe. Your standing with them decides
  which classes you unlock, which factions accept you and which paths remain
  hidden.
- **A world that breathes.** Day and night, moon cycles, eclipses and auroras.
- **Guild warfare.** Conquer territories and defend them.
- **Secrets for those who search.** Hidden classes and mysteries that reveal
  themselves only to the persistent.

## Descripción larga

Modern MUD es lo que pasa cuando creces leyendo mundos y un día decides
construir uno. Es un MUD de verdad: un mundo persistente y compartido que se
cuenta en texto y que sigue vivo cuando te desconectas. Los vecinos tienen sus
horarios, la taberna nunca cierra y cada sala esconde algo para quien se pare a
mirar. Pero no es un MUD de los de antes: tiene pixel art, cinemáticas, sonido,
interfaz y alma de juego actual.

No te lleva de la mano. Te deja un pueblo, una isla, una ciudad endeudada hasta
el cuello, y que descubras tú lo que pasa. Hay gente que le ha echado más de 70
horas y sigue pidiendo más, y eso, para un proyecto que hace una sola persona
después de su jornada, lo dice todo.

Es denso, es lento cuando tiene que serlo y premia la curiosidad. No es para
todo el mundo, y está bien así. Es para quien todavía se emociona cuando un
texto le hace imaginar más de lo que le enseña.
